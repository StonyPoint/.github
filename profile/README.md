# Stony Point, Inc.

<img src="stony-point-logo.svg" alt="Stony Point" width="160" />

Stony Point, Inc. builds the Digadop family of Salesforce products, and trains Salesforce
teams. Stony Point, Inc. does business as Digadop.

## What we do

- **Software**: the Digadop product family, at [digadop.ai](https://digadop.ai/). See the
  [Digadop organization profile](https://github.com/digadop-ai) for the full product list.
- **Training**: instructor led Salesforce training, at [stonyp.com](https://www.stonyp.com/).
